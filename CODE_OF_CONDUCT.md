# Code of Conduct

Be decent. We're a small open-source project; the bar for participation is:

- **Be kind to each other.** Disagreement is fine; personal attacks are not.
- **Critique the code, not the person.** "This breaks X" beats "you broke X".
- **Assume good intent** until you have evidence otherwise.
- **No harassment, discrimination, or threats** of any kind.

Maintainers reserve the right to remove comments, lock issues, or block users
who repeatedly violate the spirit of these rules. We'll be reasonable about it.

To report an incident, email <hello@journely.me>.
